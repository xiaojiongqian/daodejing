# 儿童道德经第七章：其二 最后面的人，最前面的心

## 第1页

有一家户外用品公司叫巴塔哥尼亚。创始人伊冯·乔伊纳德起初只想给攀岩朋友做耐用的工具和衣服。

公司越做越大，他也看见一个问题：生意如果只想着多卖、多赚，也会耗伤山、河和人的生活。

## 第2页

巴塔哥尼亚选择把“保护地球”放到赚钱前面。从一九八五年起，它长期拿出销售额的百分之一，捐给保护环境的人。

这看起来像先把钱让出去。可是顾客看见，这家公司在认真守护大家共同的家。

## 第3页

二零二二年，乔伊纳德一家把公司所有权交给专门守护地球的机构。公司仍卖衣服、付工资、改进产品；赚来的钱，一部分留着继续经营，其余的拿去保护大自然。

它先问：“怎样让山、河、海和人还能继续生活？”钱像水，去浇更重要的地方。

## 第4页

顾客买到一件外套，也买到一种信任：这家公司不会把大家共同的家，当成挖完就丢的矿山。

信任一旦长出来，比广告还长久。

## 第5页

南非的曼德拉，被关在罗本岛监狱二十七年。白天，他在采石场工作，石灰岩的反光刺伤了眼睛。

如果只看他自己受的苦，他有太多理由把仇恨放在最前面。

## 第6页

出狱以后，他没有让国家跟着自己的伤口一起燃烧。他请从前看管他的狱卒来参加自己的就职典礼，也推动大家说出真相、彼此和解。

他没有忘记那些伤害，却把南非的未来放在自己的痛苦前面。这样留下来的，有他自己的生命，也有一个国家继续一起生活的可能。

## 第7页

如果总统位置变成复仇的椅子，支持者也许会痛快一阵，国家却可能一直裂下去。

曼德拉把共同未来放到前面，从前的敌人才有台阶放下武器。

## 第8页

中国的范仲淹写《岳阳楼记》，也把自己的心放到很远很大。

他写洞庭湖的阴晴风雨，写一座楼，也写一个人怎样看天下。

## 第9页

他留下“先天下之忧而忧，后天下之乐而乐”。忧的时候站在前面，享乐的时候退到后面。

一个人如果真的把天下人的忧愁和快乐放在自己前面，就不会再困在自己的得失里。

## 第10页

范仲淹也有自己的苦和乐。他被人说过坏话，也觉得孤单。

可是他不把升官、降职当成人生的全部。所以一座楼、一片湖，能装下天下人的忧愁和快乐。

## 第11页

三个故事里，钱、伤痛和得失都可能占住中心，让人把力气花在互相争上。

先把自然、国家和天下的路守住，信任才有地方生根。

## 第12页

所以“后其身而身先”，讲的是人心的因果。

后退一步，众人会看见这里没有抢夺。人一放心，就愿意靠近，也愿意跟随。

## 第13页

“不自生”，是不把自己当成生命中心。天地生养万物，却不把万物圈成自己的东西；它的生命活在万物一代代的生长里，所以能长久。

“外其身”，是不让一点得失围住自己。人走进更大的生命，留下的有平安、要做的大事、共同的生活和宽广的心，这就是“无私，故能成其私”。

## 第14页

排队、分东西、承担责任时，先看大家怎样更好，再找自己的位置。

一滴水回到河里、海里，会跟一直流动的生命一起长久。

# Chapter 007-2: The Last Person, the Foremost Heart

## Page 1

There is an outdoor company called Patagonia. Its founder, Yvon Chouinard, simply made durable tools and clothes for climbers.

As the company grew, he saw a problem: if business only tries to sell more and earn more, it can also wear down mountains, rivers, and people's lives.

## Page 2

Patagonia chose to put protecting the planet ahead of chasing money. Since nineteen eighty-five, it has given one percent of its sales to people protecting the environment.

That looked like giving money away first. But customers saw the company working to guard a shared home.

## Page 3

In twenty twenty-two, Chouinard's family transferred ownership of the company to organizations devoted to protecting the earth. The company still sells clothes, pays workers, and improves its products. Some earnings keep the business running; the rest go to protect nature.

Its first question is, "How can mountains, rivers, seas, and people keep living?" Money flows like water to a more important place.

## Page 4

A customer buys a jacket and gains something else too: trust that this company will not treat our shared home as a mine to dig up and throw away.

When trust grows, it lasts longer than advertising.

## Page 5

Nelson Mandela in South Africa spent twenty-seven years in prison on Robben Island. By day, he worked in a stone quarry, where white limestone glare damaged his eyes.

If he had looked only at his own pain, he had many reasons to put hatred in front.

## Page 6

After leaving prison, he did not make the country burn with his wound. He invited the guards who had watched over him to his inauguration, and he helped people tell the truth and make peace with one another.

He did not forget the harm, and still he put South Africa's future before his own hurt. What was kept was his own life, and also the chance for a country to live together.

## Page 7

If the presidency had become a chair of revenge, his supporters might have felt relief for a while, but the country could have kept splitting apart.

Mandela put the shared future first, giving former enemies a way to lay down their weapons.

## Page 8

In China, Fan Zhongyan wrote "The Record of Yueyang Tower" with a heart that looked far and wide.

He wrote of the wind, rain, brightness, and darkness over Dongting Lake. He wrote of a tower, and of how a person sees the world.

## Page 9

He left the thought: be first to worry for the world, and last to enjoy its joy. Stand in front for sorrow; step behind for pleasure.

If we truly put everyone's sorrow and joy before our own gain and loss, we are no longer trapped by small wins and losses.

## Page 10

Fan Zhongyan had his own sorrows and joys. People spoke badly of him, and he knew loneliness.

But he did not make promotion or demotion the whole of life. So one tower and one lake could hold the sorrow and joy of everyone under heaven.

## Page 11

In all three stories, money, pain, and personal gain could take the center, spending people's strength on conflict.

When we put nature, our country, and the wider world first, we protect the road we share, and trust can take root.

## Page 12

"Putting oneself behind and being found in front" is a cause and effect in the human heart.

Stepping back lets others see that no one is grabbing. When people feel safe, they are willing to come close and follow.

## Page 13

"Not living for themselves" means not making the self the center of life. Heaven and earth give life to all things without claiming them as belongings. Their life continues in the growth of all things, generation after generation, so they endure.

To step outside your own gain and loss is to keep them from fencing you in. In a larger life, what endures includes your safety, the work you serve, the life you share with others, and a wider heart. This is how having no private ends can fulfill those ends.

## Page 14

When standing in line, sharing things, or taking on a job, first ask how things can go better for everyone, then find where you stand.

A drop returns to the river and the sea, lasting with the life that keeps flowing.
