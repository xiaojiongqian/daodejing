# 第018-1章：俗薄——美名出现时，先看缺口

## 原文

### 第1页

大道废，有仁义；

智慧出，有大伪；

白话：大道失去通行，仁义的名目便显得突出；机巧智谋受到推崇，重大的伪饰也随之产生。

### 第2页

六亲不和，有孝慈；

国家昏乱，有忠臣。

白话：家庭关系失去和谐，孝顺与慈爱便成为醒目的品德；国家陷入昏乱，忠臣也因此显现。

## 释义

### 第3页

第十八章像一份简短的诊断书。仁义、孝慈与忠臣集中出现时，它们指向某种失衡：日常秩序已经难以自然承载人与人的责任。

老子把目光从美名移向美名生长的条件。一个人承担了格外沉重的义务，他的品格值得尊敬，周围的制度也值得检查。

### 第4页

“大道废，有仁义”写出德目从日用转为口号的时刻。关系顺畅时，照料、守信与互助散在每天的动作里；秩序受损以后，这些动作需要被命名和倡导。

名称因此具有两重作用。它保存价值，也暴露稀缺。赞美能够鼓励个人，诊断则推动环境修复。

### 第5页

“智慧出，有大伪”针对炫示机巧的风气。语言、指标与程序都能服务真实判断，也能替空洞成果装上外表。技巧越精密，验证越需要贴近事实。

这一章邀请读者多问一步：谁在付出？谁获得名声？哪些条件让诚实行动变得艰难？答案往往藏在流程、资源与权责之中。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**俗薄：** 河上公为第十八章所加的章题，指世俗风气趋于浇薄。经文借突出德目反照社会关系的耗损。

**大道废，有仁义：** 河上公认为大道兴盛时，孝慈与仁义自然运行，名目保持平常；大道衰微以后，仁义受到专门标举。

**智慧出：** 部分版本作“慧智出”。这里的“智慧”偏向机巧与谋略。王弼关注巧智被抬高以后，伪饰也获得活动空间。

### 第7页

**六亲：** 通常指父子、兄弟、夫妇等亲属关系，也可泛指家庭成员。关系和顺时，照料融入日常；关系失衡时，孝慈成为显著称号。

**忠臣：** 忠诚在昏乱中接受严峻考验，因此格外醒目。经文同时把视线引向国家秩序本身。

**有：** 四句中的“有”既写出现，也写显现。德目被看见的同时，环境中的缺口也被照亮。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

一家照护中心准备年度表彰会。大厅里立着一块展板，照片上的护士何雨穿着值班服，标题写着：“守护到底的人。”

过去三个月，她多次延长值班，替临时缺岗的同事照看住户。管理层决定向她颁发奉献奖。

### 第9页

表彰会前一晚，何雨完成交班。她逐项核对药品、饮食和夜间观察记录，又在排班表上圈出四个空档。

护理主任走来问她是否准备好发言。

何雨说：“我准备了两张纸。一张致谢，一张排班记录。”

### 第10页

第二天，掌声响起。何雨接过奖牌，读完对同事的感谢，便把致谢折好压在奖牌下面，再展开那张排班记录。

“这些晚上由同一批人连续承担。”她说，“住户需要稳定照护，同事也需要稳定交接。请把替补名单、培训时间和临时增援写进下月计划。”

### 第11页

会议室安静下来。护理主任把排班表投到墙上，大家沿着日期逐格检查。人事主管标出招聘进度，财务人员核算临时增援，护士们补充每个时段的实际工作量。

一枚奖牌由此带出一条工作链。个人付出获得承认，机构责任也进入议程。

### 第12页

接下来的六周，中心建立替补小组，固定交班时间，并把新员工培训放进带薪排班。每次临时调度都留下记录，周会上共同复核。

何雨开始按时离开。她下班前把钥匙交给夜班护士，对方接过记录本，能够清楚说出每位住户的安排。

### 第13页

下一年的表彰会仍然举行。奖项增加了一项“流程改进”，获奖者是一组护士、排班员和培训负责人。展板上列出他们改过的表格与交接步骤。

何雨坐在台下鼓掌。她的奖牌放在值班室抽屉里，旁边压着更新后的替补名单。

### 第14页

家庭中也会出现相似情形。一个子女长期照护长辈，亲友称赞他的孝心。赞美表达感激，照护分工则继续追问时间、费用与休息如何共同承担。

孝慈进入日常安排以后，责任能够分担，照护也能持续。美德在这里获得制度支持。

### 第15页

职场常把危机中的付出称为英雄行为。表彰能够记录勇气，也可以成为审视工作条件的入口。管理者从加班、救火与个人垫付中读取信号，据此修复人员、预算和流程。

第十八章给赞美增加了一项责任：每当一个美名格外响亮，我们也去寻找托住普通生活的办法。

## 回扣

### 第16页

“大道废，有仁义”保存了仁义的价值，也揭示它被高声呼唤的背景。“六亲不和，有孝慈”与“国家昏乱，有忠臣”沿着同一条线，把个人品格放回关系结构。

老子的警觉落在因果上。显眼的德行值得敬重，产生它的困境值得处理。

### 第17页

夜班开始，值班室里进行一次平稳交接。记录本从一只手传到另一只手，药柜钥匙随之交付。何雨穿过大厅，展板已经换成住户的春季活动表。

照护继续发生，责任由许多人共同承担。仁义回到日用，也回到每一张排班表。

当美名照见缺口，修复便有了起点。

# Chapter 018-1: Customs Grown Thin—When Virtue Becomes a Signal

## Original Text

### Page 1

When the great Dao is abandoned, benevolence and righteousness appear.

When clever wisdom emerges, great hypocrisy appears.

### Page 2

When the family is out of harmony, filial devotion and parental care appear.

When the state falls into disorder, loyal ministers appear.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Eighteen reads like a brief diagnosis. When benevolence, filial devotion, and loyalty become conspicuous, they point toward an imbalance: ordinary order has ceased to carry human responsibility with ease.

Laozi turns our attention from the honored name to the conditions that produced it. A person carrying an exceptional burden deserves respect, and the surrounding institution deserves examination.

### Page 4

“When the great Dao is abandoned, benevolence and righteousness appear” marks the moment when daily virtues become public slogans. In healthy relations, care, trust, and mutual aid are dispersed through ordinary actions. Under strain, they require names and exhortation.

A name therefore performs two tasks. It preserves a value and reveals scarcity. Praise encourages a person; diagnosis begins repairing the setting.

### Page 5

“When clever wisdom emerges, great hypocrisy appears” addresses a culture that displays ingenuity. Language, metrics, and procedure can serve honest judgment, and they can dress an empty result in convincing form. Sophisticated technique calls for verification close to the facts.

The chapter asks us to take one further step: Who is carrying the burden? Who receives the reputation? Which conditions make honest action difficult? The answers often lie in process, resources, authority, and responsibility.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Customs Grown Thin:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Eighteen. It refers to the thinning of shared customs and relations. Conspicuous virtues reflect the wear within social life.

**When the great Dao is abandoned, benevolence and righteousness appear:** Heshang Gong says that when Dao flourishes, care and right conduct operate naturally and their names remain ordinary. As Dao declines, they require special proclamation.

**Clever wisdom emerges:** Some textual versions reverse the two characters for wisdom. Here the phrase leans toward ingenuity and strategic cleverness. Wang Bi observes that when cleverness is elevated, disguise gains room to operate.

### Page 7

**The family:** The Chinese term traditionally covers relations among parents, children, siblings, and spouses. In harmony, care enters routine. Under strain, filial devotion and parental care become distinguished titles.

**Loyal ministers:** Loyalty becomes especially visible when disorder puts it under severe pressure. The line directs attention toward the condition of the state as well as the character of the minister.

**Appear:** The repeated verb means both to arise and to become visible. As a virtue enters view, a gap in its surroundings is illuminated.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

A residential care center prepares its annual awards ceremony. In the lobby stands a display with a photograph of a nurse named He Yu. The heading reads, “The One Who Stayed Until the End.”

During the past three months, she has often extended her shifts to cover temporary vacancies. Management plans to give her an award for dedication.

### Page 9

On the evening before the ceremony, He Yu completes a handover. She checks medication, meals, and overnight observations, then circles four gaps on the staffing roster.

The director of nursing asks whether her speech is ready.

“I have two pages,” He Yu says. “One for thanks, and one for the roster.”

### Page 10

Applause fills the room the next day. He Yu accepts the plaque and reads her thanks to colleagues who covered one another during busy hours. She folds that page beneath the plaque and opens the staffing record.

“The same group carried these nights in succession,” she says. “Residents need continuity of care, and colleagues need continuity of handover. Please place a reserve list, training hours, and temporary support in next month's plan.”

### Page 11

The meeting room grows quiet. The nursing director projects the roster, and the group checks every date. Human resources marks the progress of recruitment, finance calculates temporary support, and nurses add the actual workload for each period.

One plaque reveals an entire chain of work. Personal effort receives recognition, and institutional responsibility enters the agenda.

### Page 12

Over the next six weeks, the center forms a reserve group, fixes the hours for handover, and places new-staff training inside paid shifts. Every emergency adjustment enters a record for weekly review.

He Yu begins leaving on time. Before each departure, she passes the keys to the night nurse, who receives the record book and can explain each resident's plan.

### Page 13

The following year's ceremony continues. A new award recognizes improvement in process, and it goes to a group of nurses, schedulers, and training staff. Their display shows the forms and handover steps they revised.

He Yu applauds from the audience. Her own plaque rests in a duty-room drawer beside the updated reserve list.

### Page 14

Families encounter similar conditions. One adult child may care for an aging parent over many years and receive praise for devotion. Gratitude honors the work, while the division of care continues to ask how time, cost, and rest can be shared.

When filial care enters the daily arrangement, responsibility can circulate and care can endure. Virtue gains institutional support.

### Page 15

Workplaces often call extraordinary effort during crisis heroic. Recognition can preserve courage and open an examination of working conditions. Leaders read overtime, emergency rescues, and personal expenditure as signals, using them to repair staffing, budgets, and process.

Chapter Eighteen adds a duty to praise: whenever an honored name sounds especially loud, we also search for a way to support ordinary life.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

“When the great Dao is abandoned, benevolence and righteousness appear” preserves the value of virtue and reveals the circumstances that make virtue a public cry. The lines on family discord and state disorder continue the same movement, returning individual character to the structure of relationship.

Laozi's alertness concerns causation. Conspicuous character deserves respect, and the difficulty producing it deserves repair.

### Page 17

The night shift begins with a steady handover. A record book passes from one hand to another, followed by the medicine cabinet key. He Yu crosses the lobby, where the old display has given way to the residents' spring activity schedule.

Care continues, carried by many people. Benevolence returns to daily use and to every staffing roster.

When an honored name illuminates a gap, repair has a place to begin.
