# 第020-1章：异俗——在喧声中守住来源

## 原文

### 第1页

绝学无忧。唯之与阿，相去几何？善之与恶，相去若何？人之所畏，不可不畏。荒兮其未央哉！

众人熙熙，如享太牢，如春登台。

我独泊兮其未兆，如婴儿之未孩；儽儽兮若无所归。

白话：结束对浮华学说的追逐，忧虑也会止息。“唯”与“阿”听来相近，诚恳应答与迎合却各有用心；善恶之间也需要仔细辨认。众人共同畏惧的事，仍有现实力量。世路辽阔，仿佛漫长无边。人群兴盛欢悦，像参加盛宴，又像春日登台。我独自安静，心念停在萌芽之前，像婴儿尚在呀呀发声，也像远行者漂泊在路上。

### 第2页

众人皆有余，而我独若遗。我愚人之心也哉！沌沌兮。

俗人昭昭，我独昏昏；俗人察察，我独闷闷。

澹兮其若海，飂兮若无止。

众人皆有以，而我独顽似鄙。

我独异于人，而贵食母。

白话：众人似乎样样充足，我却像遗漏了什么。我的心如此浑厚迟钝。世人显得明白，我保持含混；世人精于分辨，我保持宽缓。心境像海一样舒展，像长风一样流动。众人都有所施展，我却显得笨拙朴陋。我珍重滋养万物的根源，因此选择了另一种生活尺度。

## 释义

### 第3页

第二十章拥有鲜明的第一人称。老子把自己放在人群旁边，听见盛宴，也感到群体标准带来的压力。他保持一段距离，观察赞同怎样滑向迎合，聪明怎样滑向竞逐。

这段距离需要代价。“若遗”“昏昏”“闷闷”“顽似鄙”都写出旁人眼中的失意与迟钝。独立判断常以失去掌声开始。

### 第4页

“唯”与“阿”都是应答声，语气和心意各有差别。一个人诚恳回应事实，另一个人顺着权势调整答案。声音相距很近，选择已经走向相反方向。

“人之所畏，不可不畏”承认社会压力的真实。离开群体节奏需要审慎，也需要承担职位、关系和名声上的后果。

### 第5页

“绝学”让外在标准的追逐停下来。学问由此回到用途，在经验中接受检验。“我独”也成为一种认知姿态：给判断保留形成时间。

篇末“贵食母”指出力量来源。“母”是生养万物的根本。回到根源，意味着回到事实、生命与基本需要，从那里获得持续滋养。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**异俗：** 河上公为第二十章所加的章题，指行道者与流俗保持差异。差异体现在价值次序与生活尺度上。

**绝学：** 河上公把这里的“学”指向浮华失真的学问。王弼则联系“为学日益、为道日损”，强调对增益与进取的反省。

**唯与阿：** 两字都可表示应答。“唯”偏向恭敬承应，“阿”带有曲从迎合之意。经文借细微声差追问应答者的真实立场。

### 第7页

**太牢：** 古代祭祀中的隆重牲礼。经文以“享太牢”写人群面对盛宴时的兴奋。

**泊、沌沌、闷闷：** 这些词共同呈现安静、浑厚和宽缓。它们也保留旁观者眼中迟钝含混的外观。

**食母：** 河上公把“食”解释为用，把“母”解释为道；王弼称“食母”为生命的根本。结尾由群体评价转回生养之源。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

一家投资机构举行年度研究会。大厅摆着长桌，屏幕轮流播放增长预测。合伙人端着酒杯交谈，每个人胸前都别着姓名牌。

研究主管程知带来一份港口物流报告。她的电脑里保存着仓库记录、运费合同和访谈纪要。

### 第9页

下午的闭门会上，各组依次汇报。多数报告使用同一组乐观假设，图表的曲线整齐向上。主持人说，市场正在等待一个清楚结论。

轮到程知时，她把预测范围留在屏幕上，也标出三项仍待核验的数据。

### 第10页

一位合伙人问：“你赞同明年的增长判断吗？”

程知回答：“现有订单支持前两个季度。后面的区间取决于续约和运价。”

对方又问：“能把这句话说得积极一些吗？”

“我可以把条件说得清楚。”她说。

### 第11页

主持人把程知的文件移入观察项，下一份报告随即上屏。晚宴开始时，几位同事围在主桌讨论新项目。程知坐在靠门的位置，打开电脑回复港口调查员的邮件。

一名年轻分析师端着餐盘坐到她旁边：“你的结论会让项目推迟。”

程知把原始记录转给他：“先从第三页的续约表看起。”

### 第12页

两人沿着合同日期逐项核对。他们发现一批短期订单被模型当成长期收入。年轻分析师重新计算区间，又把数据来源写进脚注。

第二天，团队调整投资节奏，先等待续约结果。程知负责每周更新港口记录，也邀请运营人员参加下一次评审。

### 第13页

这次选择带来一段尴尬时间。其他项目迅速获得讨论，程知的报告停在观察名单。她仍按周打电话，听仓库经理说明货物流向，再把变化写进模型。

数月以后，续约结果到来。团队依照预先写明的条件作出决定，过程保持平稳。

### 第14页

《史记·儒林列传》记载，辕固生告诫公孙弘“务正学以言，无曲学以阿世”。这句话把学问与迎合放在同一个选择中：知识进入言说，也进入说话者如何面对权势。

第二十章中的“唯”与“阿”同样提醒我们，应答的声音很短，立场会在长期行动中显现。

### 第15页

职场里的盛宴具有多种形式：行业共识、热门指标、晋升语言和集体乐观。它们能够提供协作，也会形成速度。一个成熟团队会为异议安排正式入口，让证据获得讨论时间。

“贵食母”在专业判断中，就是珍重信息来源、工作目的和受影响的人。判断由此持续获得滋养。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第二十章把独立写得朴拙。老子站在熙熙人群旁边，承认畏惧，承认孤单，也保留自己的节奏。

他的差异拥有清楚方向：从外在评价回到生养根本，从快速应答回到真实立场。所谓“愚”，保护了尚在形成的判断。

### 第17页

研究会结束以后，程知摘下姓名牌，夹进报告最后一页。第二天清晨，她收到港口传来的新记录，又在预测区间旁写下日期。

大厅的掌声已经散去，数据仍在变化。她沿着来源继续追问，也沿着职责继续工作。

喧声之中，根本始终供应方向。

# Chapter 020-1: Different from Custom—Keeping Faith with the Source

## Original Text

### Page 1

End the pursuit of learning, and anxiety will cease. How far apart are a respectful yes and a flattering assent? How far apart are good and bad? What others fear must also be treated with care. How vast and unfinished the distance remains.

The multitude are radiant, as if enjoying a great feast or climbing a terrace in spring. I alone am quiet, before any sign has appeared, like an infant before it has learned to smile; weary, as though I had nowhere to return.

### Page 2

Others all seem to have more than enough, while I alone appear to have lost something. Mine is the mind of a fool—so undivided.

Ordinary people are bright; I alone am dim. Ordinary people are sharply discerning; I alone am broad and slow.

Calm like the sea, drifting like a wind without rest.

Others all have some purpose to display; I alone seem stubborn and uncultivated.

I alone differ from others, and value being nourished by the Mother.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Twenty speaks in a vivid first person. Laozi stands beside the crowd, hears the feast, and feels the pressure of collective standards. He keeps enough distance to watch agreement slide into flattery and intelligence into competition.

That distance has a cost. Appearing abandoned, dim, slow, stubborn, and uncultivated describes how independence looks from the outside. Independent judgment often begins with the loss of applause.

### Page 4

The two Chinese words *wei* and *a* are both sounds of assent, yet their tone and intention differ. One responds sincerely to what is true; the other adjusts its answer to power. The sounds stand close together while the choices move in different directions.

“What others fear must also be treated with care” acknowledges the force of social pressure. Departing from the collective rhythm requires prudence and a willingness to carry consequences in position, relationship, and reputation.

### Page 5

“End the pursuit of learning” allows the chase after external standards to stop. Learning can then return to use and submit to experience. “I alone” becomes a cognitive stance: giving judgment time to form.

The final phrase, “nourished by the Mother,” names the source of strength. The Mother is the origin that sustains living things. Returning to it means returning to facts, life, and basic needs, where judgment finds continuing nourishment.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Different from Custom:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Twenty. The person of Dao maintains a difference from prevailing custom, expressed through an order of value and a scale of life.

**End learning:** Heshang Gong takes the learning in question to be ornate learning that has lost truth. Wang Bi connects the line with the contrast between accumulation in learning and reduction in following Dao.

**Respectful yes and flattering assent:** Both words can answer another person. The first suggests respectful acceptance; the second carries the sense of bending toward approval. A slight difference in sound opens an inquiry into position.

### Page 7

**The great feast:** The term *tailao* refers to an elaborate sacrificial offering. The chapter uses it to evoke the excitement of a crowd before abundance.

**Quiet, undivided, broad and slow:** These terms present composure, wholeness, and spaciousness. They also preserve the appearance of dullness to an outside observer.

**Nourished by the Mother:** Heshang Gong understands nourishment as use and Mother as Dao. Wang Bi calls the Mother the root of life. The ending turns from collective judgment toward the source of sustenance.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

An investment firm holds its annual research gathering. Long tables fill the hall, while screens rotate through forecasts of growth. Partners speak over drinks, each wearing a name badge.

Research director Cheng Zhi brings a report on port logistics. Her computer contains warehouse records, freight contracts, and interview notes.

### Page 9

During the closed session, each group presents in turn. Most reports use the same optimistic assumptions, and their charts rise in orderly lines. The moderator says the market is waiting for a clear conclusion.

When Cheng Zhi's turn arrives, she keeps a range on the screen and marks three data points that still require verification.

### Page 10

A partner asks, “Do you agree with the growth forecast for next year?”

“Current orders support the first two quarters,” Cheng Zhi replies. “The later range depends on renewals and freight rates.”

“Can you phrase that more positively?”

“I can state the conditions clearly.”

### Page 11

The moderator moves Cheng Zhi's file to the watch list, and the next report appears on screen. When dinner begins, several colleagues gather at the main table to discuss new projects. Cheng Zhi sits near the door and opens her computer to answer an email from a port researcher.

A junior analyst joins her with his plate. “Your conclusion will delay the project.”

She sends him the source file. “Begin with the renewal table on page three.”

### Page 12

They check the contract dates line by line. A set of short orders has entered the model as long-term revenue. The younger analyst recalculates the range and places the source in a footnote.

The next day, the team stages its investment and waits for renewal results. Cheng Zhi updates the port record each week and invites operations staff to the following review.

### Page 13

The choice brings an awkward interval. Other projects quickly attract discussion while Cheng Zhi's report remains on the watch list. She continues her weekly calls, listens as warehouse managers describe the movement of goods, and enters each change in the model.

Several months later, the renewal results arrive. The team acts according to conditions written in advance, and the process remains steady.

### Page 14

The *Records of the Grand Historian* tells how the elder scholar Yuan Gusheng advised Gongsun Hong to speak from upright learning and never bend learning to flatter the age. The warning places knowledge and approval within a single choice: learning enters speech, along with the speaker's relation to power.

Laozi's two sounds of assent offer the same reminder. An answer takes only a moment; its position becomes visible through continued action.

### Page 15

The workplace has many kinds of feast: industry consensus, fashionable metrics, promotion language, and collective optimism. They can support cooperation and create speed. A mature team gives dissent a formal entrance and evidence enough time for consideration.

In professional judgment, valuing nourishment from the Mother means valuing sources, purpose, and affected lives. Judgment continues to receive sustenance from them.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Twenty gives independence an awkward appearance. Laozi stands beside the radiant crowd, acknowledges fear and solitude, and preserves his pace.

His difference has a direction: from external judgment to the source of life, and from rapid assent to an honest position. What looks like foolishness protects a judgment still taking shape.

### Page 17

After the gathering, Cheng Zhi removes her name badge and places it inside the final page of the report. The next morning, a new port record arrives, and she writes its date beside the forecast range.

The applause in the hall has faded; the data continues to change. She follows the source with further questions and follows her responsibility with further work.

Within the noise, the root continues to supply direction.
