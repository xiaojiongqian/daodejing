# 第015-1章：显德——清明在等待中形成

## 原文

### 第1页

古之善为士者，微妙玄通，深不可识。

夫唯不可识，故强为之容。

豫焉若冬涉川，犹兮若畏四邻，俨兮其若容，涣兮若冰之将释，敦兮其若朴，旷兮其若谷，混兮其若浊。

白话：古时善于行道的人幽深通达，外表难以测知。老子只好借一组姿态描写他们：过冬河般谨慎，面对四邻般警觉，待客般庄重，融冰般舒展，朴木般厚实，山谷般宽广，浑水般含藏。

### 第2页

孰能浊以静之徐清？

孰能安以久动之徐生？

保此道者不欲盈，夫唯不盈，故能蔽不新成。

白话：谁能让浑水安静下来，使它逐渐澄清？谁能在安定中保持长久运动，使生机逐渐发生？守住这条道的人追求适度，保留旧痕，也保留继续生成的余地。

## 释义

### 第3页

第十五章描写一种成熟人格。老子避开抽象定义，连续写出身体姿态：脚步怎样落下，人在四邻之间怎样自处，紧张怎样融解，胸中怎样留有空间。

这些形象共同指向分寸。谨慎保留行动，庄重保留温度，朴素保留能力，含浊保留等待。一个人的深度经由这些选择显现。

### 第4页

“若冬涉川”写行动前的审慎。冬水寒冷，冰面与暗流都需要判断。过河者先确认落脚处，再把重量交给下一步。

“若畏四邻”则把警觉带入关系。行动会影响旁人，旁人的反应也会改变局势。真正的谨慎因此包含环境、后果与他人的位置。

### 第5页

后面的形象逐步放松：冰开始融化，朴木保存材性，山谷容纳来物，浑水允许泥沙停留。这种人格拥有边界，也容许变化。

“浊以静之徐清”尤其重要。清明来自杂质逐渐沉降，也来自人停止搅动。等待在这里属于判断过程。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**显德：** 河上公为第十五章所加的章题。经文从外在容貌显出内在修养，因此以“显德”概括。

**豫焉若冬涉川：** 王弼本作“豫焉”，部分通行本作“豫兮”。“豫”在此写迟疑审慎的样子，河上公以“举事辄加重慎”解释。

**俨兮其若容：** 河上公把它解释为客人面对主人时的庄重。庄重来自对场合与他人的尊重。

### 第7页

**浊以静之徐清：** 浑水静置以后，泥沙逐渐沉降。王弼以此说明处理事物时的详慎，也把“徐”解释为审慎。

**安以久动之徐生：** 安定与运动在这里彼此接续。休息恢复条件，行动让生机发生。

**不欲盈：** 王弼说“盈必溢”。河上公则把“不盈”联系到收敛荣光与功名。经文赞许一种保留余地的完成。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

早春融冰以后，山谷中的公路桥接受安全检查。上游冰块撞过桥墩，河水仍带着泥色。桥的另一端连接医院、学校和一片居民区，每多关闭一天，绕行都要增加两个小时。

负责检查的工程师沈岚在清晨抵达。地方负责人已经准备好重新开放的公告，只等她签字。

### 第9页

沈岚先沿检修梯下到桥腹。她摸过支座边缘，用小锤逐点敲击，再核对裂缝标记。年轻工程师跟在后面，手里拿着平板电脑。

“模型显示承载力足够。”他说。

沈岚回答：“让模型和桥面再对一次话。”

### 第10页

他们在桥上布置测点，让一辆装载砂石的卡车缓慢驶过。车轮每前进一段，仪器便记录一次位移。沈岚站在护栏边，看数字变化，也看桥缝附近细屑移动的方向。

她把卡车停在中央，又让它原路返回。桥仍在承力，两个支座的回弹速度却出现差异。

### 第11页

中午，会议室里坐满了等待消息的人。医院代表说明药品运输的时间，校车负责人摊开绕行路线，商户则计算每天损失。

沈岚把检测结果投到墙上：“今天开放一条车道，限重通行。另一条车道继续检查支座。”

地方负责人问：“为什么只开一半？”

“现有证据支持一半。”她说。

### 第12页

这个决定看上去缺少圆满，却同时回应了两类事实。居民获得通路，桥梁也获得观察时间。工作人员每隔两小时记录一次位移，重型车辆继续绕行。

到傍晚，支座回弹趋于稳定。第二天的超声检查又确认一处需要更换的构件，维修范围由此变得清楚。

### 第13页

沈岚把河水装进透明量筒。刚取样时，细沙在水中翻动；会议结束后，沙粒已经沉到底部，水的上层逐渐清亮。

她把量筒放在两张检测图之间。等待本身提供了信息：桥梁的回弹需要时间，泥沙的沉降需要时间，人的判断也需要让证据各自归位。

### 第14页

《诗经》以“如临深渊，如履薄冰”写人的戒慎，《中庸》又说“凡事豫则立”。这些传统各有语境，都把准备看作行动的一部分。

现代航空、医疗和工程也使用检查清单、交叉复核与分阶段放行。专业能力通过程序进入现场，使谨慎成为可以共同执行的动作。

### 第15页

三天后，桥梁恢复双向通行。公告里列出维修完成的项目，也保留后续监测日期。沈岚在签字栏写下名字，把量筒里的清水倒回排水沟。

桥面重新传来车声。她收起工具，旧裂缝旁仍留着一道日期标记，提醒下一位检查者从哪里继续。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第十五章把修养写成一系列可观察的动作：冬涉般落脚，待客般自持，融冰般调整，浑水般等待。德由此进入行动节奏。

“徐清”要求时间，“徐生”要求行动。静使情况显出层次，动让新的条件逐步形成。

### 第17页

真正的完成常带着旧痕。修好的桥保留监测标记，成熟的判断保留修正日期，一个有经验的人也会为下一次观察留下位置。

“不欲盈”让成果停在证据能够支持的地方。行动因此获得分寸，后来也获得入口。

清明缓慢形成，人看清落脚处，才把重量交给下一步。

# Chapter 015-1: Revealing Character—Clarity Formed Through Waiting

## Original Text

### Page 1

The skilful masters of the Dao in old times, with a subtle and exquisite penetration, comprehended its mysteries, and were deep so as to elude men's knowledge. As they were beyond men's knowledge, I will make an effort to describe how they appeared.

Shrinking looked they like those who wade through a stream in winter; irresolute like those who fear all around them; grave like a guest; evanescent like ice that is melting away; unpretentious like wood that has not been fashioned; vacant like a valley, and dull like muddy water.

### Page 2

Who can make muddy water clear? Let it be still, and it will gradually become clear. Who can secure the condition of rest? Let movement continue, and life will gradually arise.

Those who preserve this method of Dao do not wish to be full. Through remaining unfilled, they can appear worn and leave room for renewed formation.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Fifteen describes mature character. Laozi sets aside abstract definition and gives us bodily attitudes: how a foot comes down, how a person stands among neighbors, how tension releases, and how the mind keeps room.

The images share a sense of measure. Caution preserves action, gravity preserves warmth, plainness preserves capacity, and muddiness preserves time for settling.

### Page 4

“Like someone crossing a river in winter” describes care before action. Cold water, ice, and current all demand judgment. The traveler confirms a place to stand before transferring weight into the next step.

“As if fearing the neighbors” carries awareness into relationship. Action affects others, and their response changes the situation. Care therefore includes environment, consequence, and the positions of other people.

### Page 5

The later images begin to release tension: ice melts, plain wood keeps its grain, the valley receives what enters, and muddy water allows sediment to settle. Such character has boundaries and permits change.

“Let muddy water become clear through stillness” gives waiting a place inside judgment. Clarity arrives as particles settle and the hand stops stirring.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Revealing Character:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Fifteen. The text makes inner cultivation visible through outward bearing.

**Like crossing a winter river:** Wang Bi's recension uses a form meaning cautious hesitation. Heshang Gong explains it as adding serious care whenever one acts.

**Grave like a guest:** Heshang Gong associates the line with the composure of a guest before a host. Gravity grows from respect for occasion and people.

### Page 7

**Muddy water gradually clearing:** Sediment settles when water rests. Wang Bi links the image with careful handling of affairs and glosses gradualness as deliberation.

**Rest and movement gradually giving life:** Rest restores conditions; movement allows life to arise.

**Refusing fullness:** Wang Bi says fullness must overflow. Heshang Gong connects the phrase with restraining glory and reputation. Completion retains room.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

After the spring thaw, a road bridge in a mountain valley undergoes inspection. Ice from upstream has struck the piers, and the river still carries silt. Beyond the bridge stand a hospital, a school, and several neighborhoods. Each additional day of closure adds two hours to the detour.

The inspecting engineer, Shen Lan, arrives at dawn. A reopening announcement already waits for her signature.

### Page 9

Shen Lan descends the maintenance ladder into the bridge. She runs a hand along a bearing, taps each section with a small hammer, and checks the crack marks. A younger engineer follows with a tablet.

“The model shows sufficient capacity,” he says.

“Let the model and the bridge speak once more,” she replies.

### Page 10

They place sensors across the deck and ask a truck loaded with sand to advance slowly. Instruments record displacement at each interval. Shen Lan watches the numbers and the movement of grit near an expansion joint.

She stops the truck at midspan, then sends it back. The bridge carries the load, while two bearings recover at different rates.

### Page 11

At noon, the meeting room fills with people waiting for news. The hospital representative explains the medicine schedule, the school transport officer opens a detour map, and merchants calculate daily losses.

Shen Lan projects the results. “Open one lane today under a weight limit. Keep the second lane for inspection.”

The local official asks, “Why only half?”

“The evidence supports half,” she says.

### Page 12

The decision lacks the appearance of completion and answers two sets of facts. Residents regain a route, and the bridge receives observation time. Staff record displacement every two hours, while heavy vehicles continue around the valley.

By evening, bearing recovery becomes stable. Ultrasonic inspection the next day confirms one component for replacement, giving the repair a defined scope.

### Page 13

Shen Lan fills a transparent cylinder with river water. Fine sand turns through it at first. By the end of the meeting, grains have settled and the upper water has grown clear.

She places the cylinder between two inspection charts. Waiting has supplied information: the bridge needs time to recover, sediment needs time to settle, and judgment needs time for evidence to find its place.

### Page 14

The *Book of Songs* writes of standing by an abyss and walking on thin ice. The *Doctrine of the Mean* says preparation enables action to stand. Each tradition gives preparation a place inside action.

Modern aviation, medicine, and engineering also use checklists, cross-checks, and staged release. Expertise enters a shared procedure, making caution executable by a team.

### Page 15

Three days later, both lanes reopen. The announcement lists completed repairs and a date for continued monitoring. Shen Lan signs the form and pours the clear water from the cylinder into a drain.

Traffic returns to the deck. She packs her tools. A dated mark remains beside the old crack, showing the next inspector where to continue.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Fifteen turns cultivation into observable actions: place a foot like a winter traveler, carry oneself like a guest, adjust like melting ice, and wait like muddy water. Character enters the rhythm of conduct.

Gradual clarity asks for time; gradual life asks for movement. Stillness reveals layers, and action develops new conditions.

### Page 17

Completion often carries an old mark. A repaired bridge keeps its monitoring line, a mature judgment keeps a revision date, and an experienced person leaves a place for the next observation.

Refusing fullness stops a decision where evidence ends. Action gains proportion, and the future gains an entrance.

Clarity forms slowly. Once the footing is visible, the body gives its weight to the next step.
