# 第017-1章：淳风——让功劳回到众人

## 原文

### 第1页

太上，下知有之；

其次，亲而誉之；

其次，畏之；

其次，侮之。

白话：最高层次的治理，百姓只知道治理者存在。其次，人们亲近并赞美他。再次，人们畏惧他。再往下，人们轻慢他。

### 第2页

信不足焉，有不信焉。

悠兮其贵言。

功成事遂，百姓皆谓：“我自然。”

白话：治理者给予的信任不足，人们便会产生疑虑。优秀的治理者从容而珍惜言语。事情完成以后，百姓都说：“这是我们自己做成的。”

## 释义

### 第3页

第十七章列出四种治理关系：知其存在、亲近赞美、心怀畏惧、出言轻侮。层次越向下，权力越依赖情绪与强制来证明自身。

“下知有之”保留一种适当距离。治理者完成规则、资源与协作的安排，百姓由此专注于自己的生活。

### 第4页

“信不足焉，有不信焉”揭示信任的双向生成。发布命令的人也要信任执行者，给他们信息、权限和判断空间。承诺还要接受行动检验。

信任积累于可预测的日常：标准保持一致，职责能够追溯，问题得到回应。每一次兑现都为下一次合作铺路。

### 第5页

“贵言”要求珍惜语言。治理者减少空泛许诺，把每一句公开承诺放进预算、流程与日期。语言因此获得重量。

“我自然”则写出能力的归属。百姓在共同完成事情以后，能够说清方法、承担责任，也能在下一次继续行动。治理的成果进入了众人的手中。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**淳风：** 河上公为第十七章所加的章题。“淳”指质朴厚实，经文所写的风气让百姓保有自信与行动能力。

**太上，下知有之：** 河上公说，最高层次的君主顺应自然，百姓仅知其存在。王弼强调无为与少言，使治理痕迹保持轻微。

**亲而誉之：** 亲近与赞美已经让治理者成为注目中心。经文把这种受欢迎的治理放在第二层，提醒读者继续追问百姓获得了多少自主能力。

### 第7页

**信不足焉：** 河上公从频繁命令与烦琐禁令解释信任受损。王弼则把信看作自然发生的关系。治理者如何对待百姓，会塑造百姓如何回应治理。

**悠兮其贵言：** “悠”写从容舒缓，“贵言”写慎重发言。言语数量受到节制，承诺质量受到重视。

**我自然：** “自然”在这里指自然而然、自身如此。事情成功以后，百姓把成果理解为自己的行动所得。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

初夏，一座沿海城市发布高温预警。老城区楼房密集，许多独居老人住在高层。区应急协调员周宁提前两个月走访社区，桌上始终放着一张街区图。

图上标着图书馆、药房、诊所和公共饮水点，也标着每栋楼的联系人。

### 第9页

第一次协调会开了四十分钟。周宁问了三个问题：“谁保管公共房间的钥匙？谁熟悉楼里的老人？停电时，消息从哪里传出去？”

物业人员、社区护士和店主把答案写进表格。每项任务旁边留有姓名、联系方式和替补人选。

### 第10页

周宁把市里的高温预案缩成一张单页流程。预警发布以后，图书馆延长开放，药房提供饮水，楼栋联系人逐户问候。需要医疗帮助的人由护士评估，再联系诊所。

她带着各组演练一次。每个环节都由实际执行者复述，流程也根据现场意见修订。

### 第11页

演练结束，一位楼栋联系人问：“正式预警时，你会来到这里指挥吗？”

周宁回答：“我会守在协调台。楼里的情况由你们判断，跨部门的问题交给我。”

这句话划清了责任。熟悉住户的人贴近现场，掌握公共资源的人负责调度。

### 第12页

七月，高温持续数日。清晨七点，楼栋联系人开始敲门。图书馆打开活动室，店主把饮水箱推到门口，护士沿预定路线走访。

协调台接到一处电梯故障报告。周宁联系维修人员，又安排志愿者帮助高层住户取药。其他街区继续按流程运行。

### 第13页

傍晚，社区会议室汇总当天情况。每组报告已完成的事项，也标出明天需要调整的路线。周宁坐在桌边记录，发言始终简短。

一位居民说：“我们这栋楼已经排好明早的顺序。”这份安排空出了两个人手，另一位居民便接过任务：“药房那边由我们补水。”任务在众人之间自然衔接。

### 第14页

预警解除以后，市里举行复盘会。记者询问谁设计了这套安排。楼栋联系人指向墙上的街区图：“大家一起补全的。每个人都知道自己那一格。”

周宁提交了预算明细、调度记录和故障报告。公共职责清楚留存，行动经验则分布在社区之中。

### 第15页

这种领导方式也适用于企业与家庭。团队负责人明确目标、资源和决策边界，再让最接近问题的人选择方法。父母建立安全规则，也让孩子逐步负责自己的行程。

每一次授权都需要可追溯的责任，每一份信任都需要支持条件。“我自然”由准备、练习与真实权限共同促成。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第十七章关注权力留下的痕迹。命令充满现场时，人们等待指示；信任进入流程时，人们开始判断与协作。

“贵言”使治理者专注于关键承诺，“功成事遂”则检验制度能否把能力交给众人。百姓说“我自然”，意味着他们已经进入事情的因果链。

### 第17页

秋天，周宁再次来到老城区。那张街区图已经添上新联系人，也改过几条路线。居民自己主持会议，她坐在末席核对公共资源。

窗外传来菜市场收摊的声音。制度安静地托住日常，众人继续安排自己的生活。

最深的治理，让人记得怎样共同完成一件事。

# Chapter 017-1: Simple Customs—Returning Achievement to the People

## Original Text

### Page 1

Of the highest rulers, the people merely know that they exist.

The next are loved and praised.

The next are feared.

The next are treated with contempt.

### Page 2

Where trust is insufficient, distrust arises.

How unhurried they are, valuing their words.

When the work is accomplished and affairs are complete, the people all say, “We did it by ourselves.”

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Seventeen presents four relations between rule and the people: awareness of its existence, affection and praise, fear, and contempt. As the sequence descends, power relies increasingly on emotion and force to make itself felt.

“They merely know that the ruler exists” preserves a fitting distance. Rules, resources, and coordination are in place, leaving people free to attend to their own lives.

### Page 4

“Where trust is insufficient, distrust arises” describes a reciprocal creation. Those who issue instructions must also trust those who carry them out, giving them information, authority, and room for judgment. Promises must submit to the test of action.

Trust accumulates through predictable practice: standards remain consistent, responsibility can be traced, and problems receive a response. Each promise kept prepares the next collaboration.

### Page 5

“Valuing words” means treating speech as a scarce resource. A leader reduces vague promises and gives every public commitment a budget, a procedure, and a date. Language gains weight.

“We did it by ourselves” identifies where capacity resides. After completing the work together, people can explain the method, carry responsibility, and act again. The result of governance has entered their hands.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Simple Customs:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Seventeen. The word translated as simple suggests an unforced solidity. The political atmosphere described here lets people retain confidence and practical ability.

**Of the highest rulers, the people merely know they exist:** Heshang Gong says the finest ruler follows what is natural, so the people know only of the ruler's existence. Wang Bi emphasizes action with a light trace and restrained speech.

**Loved and praised:** Affection and praise already place the ruler at the center of attention. The text ranks popular government second, inviting a further question about how much agency the people themselves have gained.

### Page 7

**Where trust is insufficient:** Heshang Gong connects damaged trust with frequent commands and elaborate prohibitions. Wang Bi treats trust as a relation that arises naturally. A government's treatment of people shapes their response.

**Unhurried, valuing words:** The first term conveys spacious composure. Valuing words restrains their number and raises the seriousness of commitment.

**We did it by ourselves:** The term translated as “by ourselves” is *ziran*: so of itself, or so from itself. Once the work succeeds, the people understand the result as their own accomplishment.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

In early summer, a coastal city issues a heat warning. The old district has dense apartment blocks, with many older residents living on upper floors. Two months earlier, district emergency coordinator Zhou Ning began visiting the neighborhood with a street map under her arm.

It marks the library, pharmacy, clinic, and public water points, along with a contact in every building.

### Page 9

The first coordination meeting lasts forty minutes. Zhou Ning asks three questions: “Who keeps the key to the common room? Who knows the older residents in each building? During a power failure, where will information travel?”

Building staff, community nurses, and shopkeepers enter their answers in a table. Each task receives a name, a phone number, and a backup person.

### Page 10

Zhou Ning reduces the city's heat plan to a one-page sequence. Once a warning begins, the library extends its hours, the pharmacy provides water, and building contacts knock on doors. A nurse assesses anyone needing medical help and then contacts the clinic.

She leads one rehearsal. The people responsible for each step explain it in their own words, and the procedure changes in response to their experience.

### Page 11

After the rehearsal, a building contact asks, “During a real warning, will you come here and direct us?”

“I will be at the coordination desk,” Zhou Ning replies. “You judge conditions inside the building. Bring cross-agency problems to me.”

The sentence draws a clear line of responsibility. People who know the residents stay close to the situation; people with public resources handle coordination.

### Page 12

In July, the heat lasts for several days. At seven each morning, building contacts begin knocking on doors. The library opens its activity room, a shopkeeper wheels water to the entrance, and nurses walk their planned routes.

The coordination desk receives a report of a stalled elevator. Zhou Ning calls the repair team and arranges for volunteers to collect medicine for residents on upper floors. The other blocks continue through the sequence.

### Page 13

Each evening, groups gather at the community hall to review the day. They report completed tasks and mark routes for adjustment. Zhou Ning records the discussion from the side of the table and keeps her own speech brief.

“Our building has tomorrow morning's order,” one resident says. The arrangement frees two people, so another resident takes up the next task: “We will refill the pharmacy's water supply.” Tasks join across the room.

### Page 14

After the warning ends, the city holds a review. A reporter asks who designed the arrangement. A building contact points to the map. “We filled it in together. Each person knows their square.”

Zhou Ning submits the budget, dispatch log, and fault reports. Public responsibility remains legible, while practical knowledge stays distributed through the neighborhood.

### Page 15

The same leadership can guide companies and families. A team leader defines the goal, resources, and boundary of decision, then lets the people closest to the issue select the method. Parents establish safety rules while children gradually manage their own journeys.

Every grant of authority needs traceable responsibility, and every act of trust needs supporting conditions. “We did it ourselves” grows from preparation, practice, and real permission.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Seventeen studies the trace left by power. When instructions fill a scene, people wait to be directed. When trust enters the process, they begin to judge and cooperate.

Valuing words concentrates leadership on essential commitments. Completing the work then tests whether the institution has placed capacity in many hands. The people's statement means that they have entered the chain of cause and effect.

### Page 17

In autumn, Zhou Ning returns to the old district. New contacts have been added to the map and several routes revised. Residents chair the meeting while she sits at the end of the table and checks the public resources.

Outside, the market closes for the day. The institution quietly supports ordinary life, and people continue arranging their affairs.

The deepest governance leaves people remembering how they completed something together.
