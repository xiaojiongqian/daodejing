# 第012-1章：检欲——把注意力还给生活

## 原文

### 第1页

五色令人目盲，五音令人耳聋，五味令人口爽，驰骋畋猎令人心发狂，难得之货令人行妨。

白话：纷繁的颜色会扰乱视觉，密集的声音会损伤听觉，浓重的滋味会使味觉失准。纵马围猎使心神躁动，追逐珍货也会妨碍人的行为。

老子把五种感官经验排在一起，写出欲望逐步加速的过程：先被吸引，继而追逐，最后偏离原先要走的路。

### 第2页

是以圣人为腹不为目，故去彼取此。

白话：因此，圣人选择能够养护生命的事物，把单纯取悦眼目的诱惑放到次位，取前者而舍后者。

“腹”代表身体能够承受并从中得到滋养的尺度；“目”代表外物持续引发的追逐。老子关心的是欲望由谁作主，以及一个人最终为哪一种需要付出时间。

## 释义

### 第3页

五色、五音、五味本是古人概括颜色、声音与味道的方式。“五”在这里指向种类的完备。绘画、音乐与饮食都属于人的生活，也各有技艺与教养。

问题出现在刺激接连加码之后。眼睛开始追逐下一幅画面，耳朵等待下一声提示，舌头依赖下一层重味。感官仍在工作，分辨力却逐渐变钝。

### 第4页

王弼把“爽”解释为“差失”，也就是失去原有的准度。他指出，耳、目、口、心本来各有自然作用；外物若长期牵引它们，顺性便会转成伤性。

这段解释把重点放在功能的倒置上。颜色原本供人观看，声音原本供人聆听，货物原本供人使用。追逐加速以后，人开始为颜色停留，为声音守候，为货物安排生活。

### 第5页

“为腹”提供一个贴近身体的判断方法。身体会用饱足、疲劳、睡眠和疼痛说明限度；欲望则善于把每一次获得改写成下一次追求的起点。

河上公把这一章题为“检欲”。“检”含有约束与察验之意。圣人先察看一件事能否养人，再决定投入多少时间。取舍因此成为注意力的管理，也成为生活方向的选择。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**口爽：** “爽”在这里取差失之义。王弼说“失口之用”，河上公也以“亡”解释它。经文写味觉受到浓味牵引以后，逐渐失去辨别的准度。

**驰骋畋猎：** “畋猎”指田猎。传本有“畋”“田”的字形差异，基本含义相通。骑乘、追逐和围猎共同构成强烈刺激，也使心神持续处于兴奋之中。

**行妨：** 王弼说难得之货“塞人正路”。“妨”由此指行为受到阻碍，人的选择开始围绕珍货转动。

### 第7页

**为腹：** 王弼说“以物养己”。食物、器具与娱乐都回到服务生命的位置，人依照需要决定用量。

**为目：** 王弼说“以物役己”。眼前的吸引获得主导权，人随外物不断调整时间与行为。

这组词包含清楚的方向关系：物养人，或人役于物。现代生活中的屏幕、商品与娱乐拥有新的形态，这个方向问题仍然存在。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

一家内容平台召开季度产品评审。墙上并排挂着六块屏幕：短片自动播放，消息角标持续跳动，推荐卡片依次滑入。每一种变化都经过测试，目的都是让手指多停留一会儿。

负责人公布本季结果：用户停留时间上升，夜间打开次数也在增加。会议桌旁响起掌声。产品设计师林岚看着屏幕，记下另一个数字：许多用户在打开应用几分钟后，已经忘记最初想找什么。

### 第9页

下午，团队观察一场使用测试。参与者是一位经常出差的咨询顾问。他走进房间时说：“我想找上周保存的那篇睡眠文章。”

首页推来一段新闻，新闻末尾接上好友动态，动态下面又弹出限时课程。每个入口都把顾问带向下一页。他连续点开三次，等屏幕回到首页才抬起头：“我刚才准备找什么？”

记录员在表格里写下任务失败。后台却把这十分钟记作一次活跃会话。

### 第10页

林岚把两项记录放在同一页。一个指标显示增长，另一个事实显示用户偏离目的。她请团队暂时关掉屏幕上的动态图，只保留进入应用时的选择：“继续昨天的内容”“查找收藏”“浏览推荐”。

第二位参与者选择收藏，两步便找到文章。他读完以后，页面出现一句提示：“今天读到这里。”下方仍有继续浏览的入口，手指也可以停下。

这次会话只持续六分钟。参与者离开时说：“事情做完了。”

### 第11页

下一场会议里，增长负责人指着曲线：“六分钟会拉低停留时间。”

林岚问：“平台要增长什么？”

会议室里的人开始拆分那个熟悉的词。停留时间容易计算，完成一次真实目的也可以计算；即时点击带来广告收入，三个月后的主动续订同样关系收入。指标一旦改变，团队眼中的成功也会改变。

### 第12页

他们用两个星期调整产品。夜间消息汇入一份摘要，连续播放在一段内容后停住，购物入口显示当月支出，收藏页回到首页首屏。

这些设计依然提供新闻、课程与商品，也继续邀请用户探索。它们同时让开始、继续和结束都成为可见选择。

“为腹”在这里获得了具体形式：产品帮助人完成一件已经选择的事，并在完成处承认事情已经结束。

### 第13页

团队也保留了吸引人的画面。摄影师仍可呈现颜色，作曲者仍可安排声音，厨师仍可调配味道。第十二章关心刺激与人的关系，艺术本身仍有自己的价值。

界线出现在选择权转移的时刻。颜色推动下一次点击，声音召回已经离开的注意力，稀缺标签催促即时购买。人的时间由此进入外物的安排，平台则把这种服从记成增长。

### 第14页

《奥德赛》中的奥德修斯想听塞壬的歌声，也知道歌声会把船引向礁石。他让水手用蜡封耳，又让他们把自己绑在桅杆上。船驶入歌声范围时，清醒时立下的安排替他守住航向。

这个故事提供一种跨文化连接：自制可以提前进入环境。绳索、同伴与约定共同承担选择，人在诱惑抵达之前已经决定自己要去哪里。

### 第15页

现代人的“桅杆”往往很小。手机在夜间进入专注模式，购物清单在进店前写好，一顿饭在饱足时结束，一段工作在固定时间交接。每一项安排都把清醒时的决定带到欲望升高的时刻。

这些做法的价值来自具体目的。一个人知道自己准备完成什么，也知道何时离开。注意力由此回到生活，工具也回到工具的位置。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第十二章依次写目、耳、口、心与行。感官受到牵引以后，影响会进入情绪，再进入行为。老子由此把一场观看推进成一条生活路径。

“去彼取此”要求明确取舍：选择滋养，节制追逐；选择原先的目的，收回被刺激带走的时间。欲望仍可被感受，决定权则回到人手中。

### 第17页

新版应用上线后的一个夜晚，那位咨询顾问再次打开收藏页。文章读完，屏幕停在结束提示上。他把手机放到桌边，起身倒了一杯水。

厨房里传来冰箱的低鸣，隔壁房间有人翻动书页。他站着喝完水，把杯子放回沥水架，顺手关掉厨房灯。

眼睛看过，耳朵听过，生活仍沿着自己选择的方向继续。

# Chapter 012-1: Examining Desire—Returning Attention to Life

## Original Text

### Page 1

Colour's five hues from th' eyes their sight will take;
Music's five notes the ears as deaf can make;
The flavours five deprive the mouth of taste;
The chariot course, and the wild hunting waste
Make mad the mind; and objects rare and strange,
Sought for, men's conduct will to evil change.

In modern terms: a flood of color can disorder sight, dense sound can dull hearing, and heavy flavor can disturb taste. Racing after excitement agitates the mind, while rare goods begin to redirect conduct.

### Page 2

Therefore the sage seeks to satisfy (the craving of) the belly, and not the (insatiable longing of the) eyes. He puts from him the latter, and prefers to seek the former.

In modern terms: the sage chooses what nourishes life and gives visual temptation a secondary place.

The belly represents a measure the body can bear and use. The eye represents the pull of whatever appears next. Laozi asks who directs desire and where a person finally gives time.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

The five colors, five tones, and five flavors were traditional ways of naming the full range of sight, sound, and taste. Painting, music, and food all belong to human cultivation.

The difficulty begins when stimulation keeps escalating. The eye chases another image, the ear waits for another alert, and the tongue depends on another heavy flavor. The senses remain active while discernment grows dull.

### Page 4

Wang Bi explains *shuang* as a loss of accuracy. Eyes, ears, mouth, and mind each possess a natural function; prolonged external pressure can turn ordinary use into injury.

His point concerns a reversal of function. Color exists for people to see, sound for people to hear, and goods for people to use. Under continual pursuit, people begin arranging their lives around color, sound, and goods.

### Page 5

“For the belly” offers a bodily measure. Satiety, fatigue, sleep, and pain all communicate limits. Desire tends to turn each acquisition into the beginning of another pursuit.

Heshang Gong titles this chapter “Examining Desire.” Examination here includes restraint and testing. The sage first asks whether something nourishes life, then decides how much time to give it.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**The mouth loses accuracy:** Here *shuang* means deviation or loss. Wang Bi describes it as the mouth losing its proper use; Heshang Gong gives a similar reading. Heavy flavor gradually weakens the tongue's measure.

**Racing and hunting:** The image combines speed, pursuit, and the excitement of the hunt. Received versions use variant characters for *tian*, both carrying the basic sense of hunting.

**Conduct obstructed:** Wang Bi says rare goods block the proper road. Choice begins to revolve around possession.

### Page 7

**For the belly:** Wang Bi glosses this as using things to nourish oneself. Food, tools, and entertainment remain in the service of life.

**For the eye:** Wang Bi describes a person becoming subject to things. Visible attraction gains direction over time and conduct.

The relation has a clear direction: things may nourish people, or people may serve things. Screens and markets give this question new forms while preserving its force.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

A content platform holds its quarterly product review. Six screens line the wall. Short videos start on their own, notification badges jump, and recommendation cards slide into view. Each movement has been tested to keep a finger on the screen a little longer.

The director presents the results: time spent has risen, along with nighttime openings. Applause moves around the table. A product designer named Lin Lan writes down another finding: many users forget what they first came to find.

### Page 9

That afternoon, the team observes a usability session. The participant is a consultant who travels often. “I came to find the sleep article I saved last week,” he says.

The home page offers a news item. Its final line leads into a friend's update, and the update opens beneath a course with a closing deadline. Each entrance carries him toward another page. After three clicks, the screen returns him to the home page. He looks up. “What did I come here to find?”

The researcher records a failed task. The analytics system records ten minutes of active use.

### Page 10

Lin Lan places the two records on one page. One metric shows growth; the observed action shows a user losing his purpose. She asks the team to quiet the moving elements and place three choices at entry: continue yesterday's item, find a saved item, or browse recommendations.

The second participant chooses saved items and reaches the article in two steps. At the end, the page says, “You have reached the end for today.” A browsing link remains available, and the hand may also come to rest.

The session lasts six minutes. As the participant leaves, she says, “I finished what I came to do.”

### Page 11

At the next meeting, the growth director points to the curve. “Six minutes will lower time spent.”

Lin Lan asks, “What do we want the platform to grow?”

The room begins to separate meanings inside that familiar word. Time spent is easy to count; completing a chosen task can also be counted. An immediate click supports advertising revenue, while a voluntary renewal three months later also supports revenue. A change of measure changes the team's picture of success.

### Page 12

Over two weeks, the team revises the product. Night alerts enter a single digest. Continuous play pauses after one item. The shopping area shows monthly spending. Saved items return to the first screen.

News, courses, goods, and discovery remain available. Starting, continuing, and ending all become visible choices.

Here “for the belly” takes a practical form: a product helps someone complete a chosen task and acknowledges its completion.

### Page 13

The team keeps images that attract attention. A photographer may still work with color, a composer with sound, and a cook with flavor. Chapter Twelve concerns the relation between stimulus and the person. Art retains its own value.

The boundary appears when choice changes hands. Color drives another click, sound summons attention back, and a scarcity label accelerates a purchase. Human time enters the schedule of the object, while the platform records this obedience as growth.

### Page 14

In the *Odyssey*, Odysseus wants to hear the Sirens and knows that their song draws ships toward rock. He asks the sailors to seal their ears with wax and bind him to the mast. When the ship enters the range of the song, an arrangement made in clarity keeps the voyage on course.

The story offers a cross-cultural connection: self-command can enter the environment in advance. Rope, companions, and agreement carry part of the choice. A person has already chosen a destination before temptation arrives.

### Page 15

Modern masts are often small. A phone enters focus mode at night. A shopping list is written before entering the store. A meal ends at satiety. A work period reaches a scheduled handoff. Each arrangement carries a prior decision into a moment of rising desire.

Their value comes from a specific purpose. A person knows what the activity is meant to complete and when to leave. Attention returns to life, and the tool returns to its place as a tool.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Twelve moves through eye, ear, mouth, mind, and conduct. Sensory capture enters emotion and then becomes action. Laozi turns an act of looking into a path through life.

“Put away one and take the other” calls for a clear choice: choose nourishment, restrain pursuit, preserve the original purpose, and recover time taken by stimulation. Desire can still be felt; authority returns to the person.

### Page 17

One evening after the revised application is released, the consultant opens his saved items again. The article ends, and the screen rests on the closing message. He places the phone beside him and pours a glass of water.

The refrigerator hums in the kitchen. Someone turns a page in the next room. He finishes the water, returns the glass to the rack, and switches off the kitchen light.

The eyes have seen, the ears have heard, and life continues in a chosen direction.
