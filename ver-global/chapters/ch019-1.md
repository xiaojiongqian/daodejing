# 第019-1章：还淳——让制度回到本意

## 原文

### 第1页

绝圣弃智，民利百倍；

绝仁弃义，民复孝慈；

绝巧弃利，盗贼无有。

白话：止息对圣名与智巧的崇尚，百姓会获得许多实际利益；卸下仁义的标榜，家庭中的孝慈会回到日常；收起机巧与逐利，盗取利益的门路也会消退。

### 第2页

此三者以为文不足，故令有所属：

见素抱朴，少私寡欲。

白话：前面三句只写“绝弃”，表达仍然欠缺完整，因此还要指出它们的归宿：显露本色，守住朴质，减少私心，节制欲望。

## 释义

### 第3页

第十九章延续上一章的诊断。社会开始崇拜圣名、智术和利益时，名称会遮住行为，技巧也会离开生活需要。老子于是用“绝”“弃”切断这股推力。

刀锋落下以后，经文立刻给出归宿：“见素抱朴，少私寡欲。”行动回到本色，制度回到用途，欲望回到合宜尺度。

### 第4页

王弼提醒读者，圣智、仁义、巧利各有善处。经文警惕它们成为装饰、权术与诱饵。一个词越受尊崇，人越容易借用它取得声望；一套规则越靠机巧运行，普通人越依赖少数解释者。

“见素”要求内容可以直观辨认，“抱朴”要求能力留在基本结构中。二者共同降低操弄空间。

### 第5页

“少私寡欲”进一步处理动力。私欲膨胀时，聪明会寻找捷径，规则会成为猎物。欲望受到节制，技巧便能服务真实用途。

这一章关心百姓能否直接获利、家庭能否自然照料、公共资源能否稳妥抵达。判断标准始终落在生活结果上。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**还淳：** 河上公为第十九章所加的章题。“淳”指厚实本真，“还淳”表示社会风气回到朴实状态。

**绝圣弃智：** “绝”“弃”语气强烈，指向社会对圣名和智巧的追逐。河上公把“智”联系到诈伪，王弼则说明圣智本属才之善，关键在于下文所指的归宿。

**绝巧弃利：** 河上公把“巧”解释为扰乱真实的诈伪，把“利”联系到贪路与权门。经文关注利用技巧攫取利益的结构。

### 第7页

**以为文不足：** 王弼说，单凭前三句仍难显出旨意，所以经文补上“有所属”。这四个字构成重要的解释关节。

**见素：** “素”原指本色白绢，也引申为事物本色。《论语·八佾》以“绘事后素”谈底子与文饰的次序。

**抱朴：** “朴”原指原生木材。它保存材质与用途，也象征一种少受机巧改造的状态。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

城里的托育补助中心每天排起长队。申请人抱着证件和账单，在窗口之间来回核对。网站使用许多专业名词，表格又设置多层分类。

门外坐着几名代办人员。他们熟悉每条选项，也向家长收取解释费用。

### 第9页

服务设计负责人许青来到大厅，先跟着一位父亲走完整次申请。流程图留在办公室。收入栏里的术语截住了父亲的笔，他读了两遍，才拿出手机询问代办。

许青在记录本上写下三个词：谁能申请，需要什么，何时答复。

### 第10页

第二天，她召集审核员、窗口人员和技术团队。每个人拿出自己使用的表格，桌面很快铺满纸张。

许青逐项提问：“这个问题影响哪条决定？这份证明由谁核验？同一项信息出现了几次？”

团队用铅笔圈出真正参与审核的内容。

### 第11页

新版申请从一张资格说明开始。家庭先核对几项条件，再提交与决定直接相关的资料。每个术语旁边附有日常说法，受理日期与答复日期写在同一页。

审核标准也公开张贴。工作人员按照同一张清单处理，特殊情况进入复核并留下理由。

### 第12页

试运行那天，许青又跟着一位母亲走进大厅。她读完资格说明，把资料交到窗口。工作人员当场指出一处需要补充的日期，并在回执上写下查询方式。

两人走出大厅时，门外的代办桌空着。规则已经能够自己说明用途。

### 第13页

几周以后，团队举行复盘。窗口人员带来申请人最常问的句子，审核员带来复核案例，技术人员则修改网页顺序。每次新增字段都要写明用途和保存期限。

制度保持朴素需要持续工作。删减来自判断，清楚来自反复核对。

### 第14页

“绘事后素”提醒人们，色彩需要底子。公共服务也需要一层清楚底子：资格、材料、期限与责任。底子立稳，宣传、界面和技术才有附着之处。

当底子可以辨认，文饰便服务内容。普通人能够直接使用规则，专业人员也能集中处理真正复杂的情况。

### 第15页

企业中的产品流程同样如此。功能持续增加，客户会花更多时间寻找入口，员工也会围绕指标发展技巧。团队定期回到核心任务，逐项检查功能与需求的联系。

“少私寡欲”使选择获得边界。组织放下对数据、名声与增长的过量追逐，产品便能重新照顾使用者。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第十九章用强烈动词清理表层，又用“有所属”安置方向。圣智归于真实能力，仁义归于日常关系，巧利归于正当用途。

见素让人看清，抱朴让事物可用，少私寡欲让利益受到约束。四者共同构成返淳的路径。

### 第17页

傍晚，补助中心关门。窗口人员把新版清单放进明日使用的文件夹，许青在最后一栏写下修订日期。

一张表格仍会承载复杂生活。清楚的规则让家长知道下一步，也让工作人员知道自己的责任。

制度回到本意，百姓便能直接走近它。

# Chapter 019-1: Returning to Simplicity—Bringing Institutions Back to Their Purpose

## Original Text

### Page 1

End the exaltation of sages and discard cleverness; the people will benefit a hundredfold.

End proclaimed benevolence and discard righteousness; the people will return to familial care.

End cunning and discard profit; thieves will disappear.

### Page 2

These three statements, as expressions, are incomplete. Therefore let them have a place to which they belong:

Reveal the undyed cloth and embrace the uncarved wood; reduce private interest and lessen desire.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Nineteen continues the preceding diagnosis. When a society worships saintly titles, ingenious methods, and profit, names can conceal conduct and technique can drift away from human need. Laozi uses the forceful verbs “end” and “discard” to cut that momentum.

He immediately gives the clearing a destination: reveal the plain, embrace the uncarved, reduce private interest, and lessen desire. Conduct returns to its substance, institutions to their purpose, and appetite to a workable scale.

### Page 4

Wang Bi reminds us that sagely intelligence, benevolence, righteousness, skill, and benefit each possess genuine value. The text watches for their conversion into ornament, strategy, and bait. The more prestigious a word becomes, the easier it is to borrow for reputation. The more ingenious a rule becomes, the more ordinary people depend on specialist interpreters.

Revealing the plain makes content directly legible. Embracing the uncarved keeps capacity within the basic structure. Together they reduce the space for manipulation.

### Page 5

Reducing private interest and desire addresses motive. As appetite expands, intelligence searches for shortcuts and rules become prey. With appetite held to measure, skill can serve an actual use.

The chapter asks whether people receive practical benefit, families can sustain care, and public resources arrive securely. Its standard remains the result within ordinary life.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Returning to Simplicity:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Nineteen. The key word suggests an honest solidity in both character and custom.

**End the exaltation of sages and discard cleverness:** The verbs carry deliberate force and address a social pursuit of titles and ingenuity. Heshang Gong associates cleverness with deception. Wang Bi says that sagely intelligence is itself a human good, while insisting that the final lines supply the necessary direction.

**End cunning and discard profit:** Heshang Gong reads cunning as deception that confuses the genuine, and profit as a path opened to greed and privileged access. The line concerns structures that turn skill into extraction.

### Page 7

**As expressions, these are incomplete:** Wang Bi says the first three declarations alone cannot fully reveal their purpose. The instruction that they must “have a place to which they belong” is therefore crucial.

**Reveal the undyed cloth:** The Chinese word *su* originally refers to undyed silk and extends to the idea of an original ground. The *Analects* uses the phrase “painting follows the plain ground” to discuss the order between basis and adornment.

**Embrace the uncarved wood:** Unworked timber retains its material and possible uses. It becomes an image for capacity before excessive contrivance.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

Every day, a long queue forms at the city's childcare-support office. Applicants carry identity papers and bills from window to window. The website uses specialist terms, and the form divides families through many layers of classification.

Outside, application agents sit at small desks. They know every option and charge parents to interpret the rules.

### Page 9

Xu Qing, the service-design lead, enters the lobby and follows one father through the entire application. A specialist term in the income section stops his pen. He reads it twice before taking out his phone to ask an agent.

Xu Qing writes three phrases in her notebook: who qualifies, what they need, when they hear back.

### Page 10

The next day, she gathers assessors, counter staff, and the technical team. Each person brings the forms used in their own part of the process, and paper soon covers the table.

Xu Qing asks of every item, “Which decision does this affect? Who verifies this document? How many times does the same information appear?”

The team circles in pencil what the assessment actually uses.

### Page 11

The revised application begins with a single eligibility page. Families check a short set of conditions, then submit information directly tied to the decision. Everyday wording sits beside each technical term. The dates of receipt and response appear together.

Assessment standards are posted publicly. Staff work from the same checklist, while exceptional cases enter a recorded review with reasons.

### Page 12

On the first day of the trial, Xu Qing follows a mother into the lobby. She reads the eligibility page and gives her documents to the clerk. The clerk points to one date for completion and writes the inquiry method on the receipt.

As they leave, the agents' desks outside stand empty. The rules can now explain their own use.

### Page 13

Several weeks later, the team reviews the trial. Counter staff bring the questions applicants ask most often, assessors bring review cases, and developers revise the order of the website. Each proposed field must state its purpose and retention period.

Keeping an institution simple requires continuing work. Reduction comes from judgment; clarity comes from repeated checking.

### Page 14

“Painting follows the plain ground” reminds us that color needs a basis. Public service also needs a legible ground: eligibility, documents, timing, and responsibility. Once that ground is stable, publicity, interface, and technology have somewhere to attach.

When the ground is clear, presentation serves content. Ordinary people can use the rule directly, and specialists can concentrate on genuinely complex cases.

### Page 15

Product processes inside companies follow the same pattern. As features accumulate, customers spend more time searching for an entrance, while employees develop techniques around metrics. A team can return regularly to the core task and examine the connection between every feature and a real need.

Reducing private interest and desire gives choice a boundary. When an organization releases excessive pursuit of data, prestige, and growth, its product can attend once more to the people using it.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Nineteen uses forceful verbs to clear the surface and the phrase “have a place to which they belong” to establish direction. Intelligence returns to real capacity, benevolence to daily relations, and skill and benefit to legitimate use.

Revealing the plain makes things legible. Embracing the uncarved makes them usable. Reducing private interest and desire disciplines benefit. Together they form a path back to simplicity.

### Page 17

At dusk, the support office closes. A clerk places the revised checklist in tomorrow's folder, and Xu Qing writes the revision date in its final field.

One form will still carry the complexity of many lives. Clear rules show parents the next step and staff the extent of their responsibility.

When an institution returns to its purpose, people can approach it directly.
