# 第021-1章：虚心——从踪迹辨认真实

## 原文

### 第1页

孔德之容，惟道是从。

道之为物，惟恍惟惚。

惚兮恍兮，其中有象；

恍兮惚兮，其中有物。

窈兮冥兮，其中有精；

白话：盛大的德呈现何种样貌，全以道为依循。道运行于万物，恍惚而难以固定。恍惚之中含有形象，幽深之中含有实质，深远之处又含有精微力量。

### 第2页

其精甚真，其中有信。

自今及古，其名不去，以阅众甫。

吾何以知众甫之状哉？

以此。

白话：那精微力量真实可信，其中带着可以验证的效验。从今天追溯到古代，道的名义始终流传，借此可以考察万物的开端。我凭什么认识万物起始的情状？凭的正是这些持续显现的踪迹。

## 释义

### 第3页

第二十一章讨论一种特殊的认识。道超出固定形状，老子仍从“象”“物”“精”“信”逐层逼近。语言保持审慎，证据逐渐增加。

“恍惚”写边界流动，“窈冥”写来源深远。它们邀请人放松对清晰外形的执着，同时继续观察作用留下的踪迹。

### 第4页

“其中有象”提供可感轮廓，“其中有物”提供实质，“其中有精”深入到生成力量，“其中有信”则把认识落到效验。四层彼此承接。

真实因此能够通过作用得到辨认。风的形体保持自由，树叶与帆布会记录它的到来；道的尺度远为广大，万物的生成则持续提供线索。

### 第5页

“孔德之容，惟道是从”把德理解为道在生命中的呈现。人的品格服从更深秩序，行动便带着一致性。言语、选择与后果可以彼此核对。

篇末的“以此”极为简洁。老子回看万物，依据眼前生生运行的事实，追认它们共同的开端。

## 术语与考据简注

### 第6页

**虚心：** 河上公为第二十一章所加的章题。虚心使人容纳幽深经验，也使观察保持开放。

**孔德：** 河上公把“孔”解释为大，因此“孔德”指大德。王弼把“孔”联系到空，强调以空为德。两种解释分别突出广大与虚受。

**容：** 此处可理解为样貌、形态或呈现。大德的样貌依道而成。

### 第7页

**恍惚、窈冥：** 这些叠词描述幽深难定的状态。河上公说其往来无定，王弼称它“无形不系”。

**象、物、精：** 三者从轮廓进入实质，再进入精微生成。河上公以万物法象、经营生化与精实解释这组递进。

**信：** 王弼注作“信验”，强调可以印证的效验。经文中的信来自持续作用与万物生成。

## 正文

### 第8页

以下是一则原创框架（fiction）。

一座地方剧院整理旧仓库时，发现一箱织物残片。标签只剩半行年份，几块布还留着缝线。剧院把它们送到修复室，希望重新辨认原来的舞台帷幕。

织物修复师顾闻先给每块残片编号，再记录尺寸、经纬方向和接缝位置。

### 第9页

实习生把几块花纹相近的残片铺到长桌上。他试着拼出中央图案，轮廓很快出现。

顾闻看了一会儿，把每块布重新分开。

“先记录它们怎样相接。”她说，“完整图案要从证据里长出来。”

### 第10页

他们从缝线入手。针距、折边和线头方向显示出几种固定做法。剧院档案又找到一张采购单，上面写着布幅和工坊名称。

顾闻邀请一位织工来到修复室。织工用针尖分开两根经线，露出藏在下面的接头，并指出这一段采用的换线方式。

### 第11页

线索开始互相接近：接缝对应布幅，磨损对应悬挂位置，换线对应花纹转折。每一项都只说明一部分，合在一起便形成稳定关系。

顾闻把依据写进修复记录。确定程度分成几级，图案复原也标明证据来源。

### 第12页

修复团队为残片加上素色托布。原有织物获得支撑，缺处由平整底面承接。观众可以辨认整体尺度，也能看清原件与补托的边界。

实习生再次拼合图案。这一次，每条接缝都能回到记录中的依据。

### 第13页

帷幕完成安装以后，剧院保留一场公开讲解。顾闻依次介绍残片、线头、采购单和织工判断。她也说明几处仍属推测的部分。

观众听见的故事带着层次：某些信息来自材料，某些来自档案，某些来自工艺经验。可信度由来源共同支撑。

### 第14页

禅宗常说“如人饮水，冷暖自知”，强调经验要亲身领会。第二十一章又补上一条路径：万物持续生成，真实会留下效验。

个人体会与公共验证各有位置。前者进入生命，后者进入共同判断。成熟认识愿意说明自己依据了什么。

### 第15页

科学研究、商业分析与日常决策都面对恍惚阶段。问题先显出轮廓，轮廓引导材料收集；材料彼此印证，关键机制才逐渐显出，最终形成可以复核的判断。

“其中有信”要求证据链保持清楚。判断可以保留区间，依据需要接受追问。

## 回扣

### 第16页

第二十一章在幽深与真实之间建立通路。恍惚保留世界的广度，象物精信则让认识逐步落地。

大德“惟道是从”，意味着行动始终接受根本秩序校正。一个人的德由长期选择显现，道也由万物的生成获得印证。

### 第17页

演出前，工作人员拉动绳索，滑轮受力转动，修复后的帷幕随之升起。顾闻站在侧台，听见布料逐幅展开的轻响。旧残片与托布共同承受重量。

记录册留在修复室，里面写着每条线索的来处。后来的人可以沿着它重新检查，也可以继续补充。

幽深的真实，经由踪迹抵达我们。

# Chapter 021-1: Emptying the Heart—Recognizing Reality Through Its Traces

## Original Text

### Page 1

The appearance of great virtue follows Dao alone.

Dao, considered as a thing, is elusive and indistinct.

Indistinct and elusive, within it there is image.

Elusive and indistinct, within it there is substance.

Deep and obscure, within it there is essence.

### Page 2

That essence is profoundly real; within it there is verification.

From the present back to antiquity, its name has never departed, and through it we examine the beginnings of all things.

How do I know the form of those beginnings?

Through this.

## Interpretation

### Page 3

Chapter Twenty-One considers a distinctive kind of knowledge. Dao exceeds fixed form, yet Laozi approaches it through image, substance, essence, and verification. Language remains cautious as evidence gradually accumulates.

The paired words translated as elusive and indistinct describe shifting boundaries; deep and obscure describes a distant source. They loosen our attachment to a clear exterior while keeping attention on the traces left by action.

### Page 4

Image gives a perceptible outline. Substance gives material presence. Essence reaches the power of generation. Verification brings knowledge to observable effect. The four stages support one another.

Reality can therefore be recognized through what it does. Wind retains a free form, while leaves and canvas register its arrival. Dao operates on a far greater scale, and the generation of the myriad things continuously supplies clues.

### Page 5

“The appearance of great virtue follows Dao alone” understands virtue as Dao presented within a life. When character follows a deeper order, conduct acquires consistency. Speech, choice, and consequence can be checked against one another.

The final words, “through this,” are remarkably concise. Laozi looks at the living operation of things before him and, through it, recognizes their common beginning.

## Key Terms and Notes

### Page 6

**Emptying the Heart:** Heshang Gong's title for Chapter Twenty-One. An empty heart can receive deep experience while keeping observation open.

**Great virtue:** Heshang Gong interprets the first character as great. Wang Bi connects it with emptiness and emphasizes receptive capacity. The two readings bring forward breadth and openness.

**Appearance:** The term can mean countenance, form, or presentation. Great virtue takes its appearance from Dao.

### Page 7

**Elusive, indistinct, deep, and obscure:** These paired terms describe a state that is profound and resistant to fixation. Heshang Gong speaks of movement without settled form; Wang Bi calls it formless and unattached.

**Image, substance, and essence:** The sequence moves from outline to material presence and then to subtle generation. Heshang Gong explains them through the patterns of things, the formation of substance, and generative essence.

**Verification:** Wang Bi glosses the word as reliable evidence. Trust in the passage arises through continuing action and the generation of things.

## Main Text

### Page 8

The following is an original fictional framework.

While clearing an old storeroom, a regional theater finds a box of textile fragments. Half a date remains on the label, and several pieces still carry seams. The theater sends them to a conservation studio, hoping to identify the stage curtain from which they came.

Textile conservator Gu Wen numbers each fragment, then records its dimensions, weave direction, and seam position.

### Page 9

An intern lays several pieces with related motifs across a long table. He quickly assembles the outline of a central design.

Gu Wen studies it and separates the pieces again.

“First record how they join,” she says. “Let the whole pattern grow from the evidence.”

### Page 10

They begin with the seams. Stitch spacing, folded edges, and the direction of loose threads reveal a set of recurring practices. The theater archive then produces a purchase order listing the width of the cloth and the workshop's name.

Gu Wen invites a weaver to the studio. The weaver separates two warp threads with the point of a needle, exposing the join beneath them, and identifies the method used to change thread.

### Page 11

The clues begin to converge: seams correspond to cloth width, wear corresponds to hanging position, and changes of thread correspond to turns in the motif. Each item explains one part; together they establish a stable relation.

Gu Wen enters every basis in the conservation record. Degrees of certainty are marked, and each reconstructed element cites its source.

### Page 12

The team supports the fragments on a plain backing cloth. The surviving textile gains support, while open areas rest on an even ground. Viewers can recognize the overall scale and still see the boundary between the original and the support.

The intern assembles the design again. This time, every seam leads back to a recorded basis.

### Page 13

After the curtain is installed, the theater holds a public talk. Gu Wen introduces the fragments, loose threads, purchase order, and the weaver's judgment. She also identifies the areas that remain conjectural.

The story reaches the audience in layers: some information comes from material, some from archives, and some from practical craft. Reliability is carried by their sources together.

### Page 14

Chan tradition often says that the warmth or coolness of water becomes known by drinking it, emphasizing direct experience. Chapter Twenty-One adds another path: living things continually arise, and reality leaves effects.

Personal experience and public verification each have a place. One enters a life; the other enters shared judgment. Mature knowledge can explain what supports it.

### Page 15

Scientific research, commercial analysis, and daily decisions all pass through an elusive stage. A problem first acquires an outline, and the outline directs the search for material. As the material converges, a central mechanism emerges and a reproducible judgment finally takes form.

“Within it there is verification” asks for a legible chain of evidence. Judgment may retain a range, while its basis remains open to inquiry.

## Return to the Original

### Page 16

Chapter Twenty-One builds a passage between depth and reality. Elusiveness preserves the world's breadth, while image, substance, essence, and verification bring knowledge gradually to ground.

Great virtue follows Dao by allowing conduct to remain answerable to fundamental order. Character appears through choices over time, and Dao receives confirmation through the generation of things.

### Page 17

Before the performance, a stagehand pulls the rope. The pulleys take the load and raise the restored curtain. Gu Wen stands in the wings and hears the cloth unfold panel by panel. Old fragments and supporting fabric carry the weight together.

The record book remains in the studio, with the source of every clue written inside. Later readers can repeat the examination and continue it.

Profound reality reaches us through its traces.
